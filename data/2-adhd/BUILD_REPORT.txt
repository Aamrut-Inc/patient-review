This build exists to test one question: would writing custom Synthea modules have
produced better profiles than generate-a-population-and-filter? Two modules were
written, targeting the two limitations recorded as most damaging in the base build.


================================================================================
PART A - WHAT WAS BROKEN, AND WHAT THE MODULES FIX
================================================================================
A.1  ADULT ADHD
     Stock attention_deficit_disorder.json states plainly: "Althought ADHD is
     possible in adults we only model this in children." Its condition code is
     192127007 "Child attention deficit disorder", onset is a Delay of 7-15 years,
     and an Age_Guard transitions the patient to ADHD_Ends at 18.

     Consequence in the base build: P01 HAD to be 16 years old, paired with a
     young-ADULT (<30) LifeSnaps wearable trace, and several spec questions
     (nighttime eating, caloric deficit, training for an event) sit oddly on a
     16-year-old.

     modules/adult_adhd.json keeps ADHD active into adulthood using SNOMED
     406506008, with a 4.4% adult prevalence gate, childhood onset, an adult
     confirmation encounter and annual review. RESULT: P01 is now 28.

A.2  WEIGHT TRAJECTORY
     Stock Synthea records a weight at wellness encounters but produces no
     deliberate trajectory. P02 and P03 are both built around a WEIGHT PLATEAU
     which, in the base build, appeared NOWHERE in the data - it could only be
     asserted in prose. That was recorded as the single largest limitation.

     modules/weight_trajectory.json emits monthly Body Weight (LOINC 29463-7) and
     BMI (39156-5) over 24 months: 8 months of loss at about -0.9 kg/month, then a
     PLATEAU at about -0.03 kg/month. RESULT: all three profiles now carry a real,
     dated, correctly-coded weight series that shows the plateau.

A.3  MEDICATION TIME-OF-DAY (partial)
     The prescription schema across all 242 stock modules is dosage.{amount,
     frequency, period, unit}, duration, refills, as_needed - NO clock-time field.
     But `instructions` accepts SNOMED timing qualifiers and IS exported to FHIR
     (FhirR4.java:2426). The ADHD module sets it, so P01's methylphenidate now
     carries "Every morning (qualifier value)" through to the FHIR bundle.
     This is CODED timing, not a clock time - an exact "08:00" remains impossible.


================================================================================
PART B - GMF CAPABILITIES USED (and two that were checked and rejected)
================================================================================
  SetAttribute.expression   CQL-lite, syntax "#{attribute}" (State.java:741).
                            Supported by the engine, used by ZERO stock modules.
                            Drives the weight arithmetic.
  Observation.attribute     Emits a stored attribute as an Observation value.
  Guard + condition_type    "And" of Age >= 25 and Date year >= 2019, so the
                            trajectory OVERLAPS the 2021-22 wearable windows.
                            Without the date gate it fires whenever the patient
                            turns 25 and can land a decade before the window.
  Counter / conditional_transition   the monthly loop and the loss/plateau switch.

  REJECTED: VitalSign.expression. The intent was to drive Synthea's own "Weight"
  vital sign from the trajectory so its wellness-encounter weights could not
  contradict the curve. The field exists (State.java:1855) but the expression
  evaluates once rather than per pass, freezing the whole series at its seed value.
  Reverted; the interleaving is handled in post-processing instead (see D.2).

  REJECTED: -d replacing the stock module set. It does not - Module.addModules()
  ADDS to it, so these patients keep the full stock comorbidity model.

D.2  THE INTERLEAVING PROBLEM, AND HOW IT IS HANDLED
     Synthea records its own weight at wellness encounters under the SAME LOINC
     29463-7, and those points follow its independent growth model - they rise
     while the trajectory falls. Raw example from a test patient:

         2018-02-19  75.20   <- module
         2018-03-07  80.60   <- SYNTHEA, contradicts
         2018-03-21  74.30   <- module

     The builder isolates the monthly cadence, then removes strays with an
     interpolation test: a true point sits within 1.0 kg of the midpoint of its
     neighbours, because the curve moves at most 0.9 kg/month. Applied iteratively.
     COST: it also drops a few legitimate points, which is why the series are 21-25
     long rather than a full 25. All three final series decline monotonically.

D.3  FHIR
     3 bundles, all validated as R4B documents:
       P01 526 resources (26 Body Weight observations)
       P02 498 resources (22)
       P03 561 resources (24)
     The trajectory is exported as dated Body Weight and BMI Observations plus a
     summary Observation carrying start/end/loss-rate/plateau-rate components, and
     the Composition gains a "Weight trajectory" section.


================================================================================
PART E - LIMITATIONS
================================================================================
E.1  Medication time-of-day is a SNOMED qualifier, not a clock time. An exact
     dosing time remains impossible - the schema has no field for it.
E.2  SNOMED codes could not be validated offline; Synthea ships no terminology
     file. 406506008 and 307145004 should be checked against a terminology server.
E.3  The stray-removal filter is a heuristic and discards some real points.
E.4  Occupation, training, caffeine, diet and family history remain unmodelled. A
     module could only supply them by inventing content wholesale, which would
     discard the epidemiological grounding that is Synthea's whole value.
E.5  Different patients from the base build. Same archetypes and the same LifeSnaps
     wearable traces, but the clinical records are NOT the same people - the cohorts
     were regenerated with a different module set and age ranges.
E.6  The wearable half is unchanged, so every wearable limitation from the base
     build still applies (P02 capped at 77 days, P01 has 33 stress-score days, etc).

F.1  PromptEHR was NOT regenerated - it has no module system, so neither module

F.2  No module was written for occupation, training, caffeine or diet (see E.4).
F.3  The base build was not modified or replaced; this is an additional variant.
F.4  Evaluation questions, rubric and harness remain unwritten.
